👋 Welcome to BlockDuino

    

      To upload code directly to your Arduino from this browser, you'll need to install the BlockDuino Helper on your computer. It's a tiny program that compiles your code.
    

    

      You can still use BlockDuino without it — just download your sketch as a .ino file and upload it through the Arduino IDE.
